    Number 2. General Tech BleepingComputer

    Hackers exploit info disclosure bug in Gravity SMTP WordPress plugin

    WordPress sites using the Gravity SMTP plugin (≈100,000 sites) are at risk from CVE‑2026‑4020, a medium‑severity, unauthenticated information‑leak flaw that exposes a REST endpoint returning a full “System Report.” The report can reveal API keys, third‑party email credentials, WordPress configuration, server and database details, and other sensitive data, enabling attackers to impersonate the site and plan further exploits. Defiant’s Wordfence firewall has blocked over 17 million attack attempts, with a spike of 4 million blocked requests on June 7, and recommends blocking the IPs listed in their advisory and the `/wp-json/gravitysmtp/v1/tests/mock-data` endpoint. The vulnerability is fixed in Gravity SMTP 2.1.5 (released March 17); site owners should upgrade immediately. In a related alert, CVE‑2026‑8713 affects the Avada Builder plugin, allowing unauthenticated arbitrary file deletion via a path‑traversal flaw when a published Avada form stores submissions in the database; the issue is patched in Avada 3.15.4 and should be applied promptly, even though no active exploitation has been seen.

    Number 3. General Tech The Register

    Microsoft accidentally kills epic Outlook email threads

    Microsoft’s latest build of Outlook for macOS (v16.110, build 26061317) has broken a fundamental feature: when users reply to an email, the original message no longer appears in the reply window. This omission hampers threaded conversations and has already prompted complaints that the update slipped past quality checks. The only workaround, per a forum moderator, is to roll back to a previous version and disable auto‑updates until a fix arrives—an option that is impractical for managed fleets. While users can copy‑paste the relevant text, the disruption reveals a lapse in Microsoft’s testing process, especially troubling given the company’s recent push to phase out Office 2019 on Macs and its broader strategy of encouraging upgrades. The Register has yet to receive a response from Microsoft regarding the issue.

    Number 5. General Tech Ars Technica

    Trump admin’s coal investments assist plants with repeated violations

    In 2023, Tennessee’s Cumberland Fossil Plant—already plagued by pollution, equipment failures and health risks—was slated to shut its units in 2026 and 2028 after a 2011 settlement that required the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) to install pollution controls. Yet, following a Trump‑era reshuffle of TVA’s board, the agency secured a $46 million federal grant to extend the plant’s life—one of only three DOE‑funded coal plants repeatedly cited for Clean Air and Water Act violations, alongside Oklahoma’s Grand River Energy Center and North Carolina’s Roxboro Steam Electric Plant. Local residents like Angie Mummaw view the extension as a setback to clean‑energy progress, while clean‑energy advocates such as Maggie Shober argue that keeping coal plants running accelerates climate change and health harms. Studies link the plant’s toxic fine‑particle emissions to roughly 1,000 premature deaths across the Northeast from 1999‑2020, underscoring the broader public‑health impact of continued coal operations.

    The UK Just Lost Its Sixth Prime Minister of the Decade

    UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer resigned less than two years after leading Labour to a landslide victory, citing his party’s assessment that he is not the best fit to guide them into the next election. His popularity has plummeted amid tax rises, spending cuts, and unmet public‑service promises, with May’s local elections seeing Labour lose 1,500 council seats and many councils shift to the Reform UK party. Starmer’s resignation comes as Andy Burnham, newly elected MP and former Manchester mayor, is poised to succeed him, with former Health Secretary Wes Streeting pledging support. The resignation follows a series of high‑profile leadership changes since the 2016 Brexit referendum, and Starmer will remain in office until the Labour Party elects a new leader, expected before Parliament reconvenes in September.
